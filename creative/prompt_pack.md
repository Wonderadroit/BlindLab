# BlindLab Photorealistic Prompt Pack

## Master style

> Photorealistic cinematic commercial photography, real-world location, authentic human proportions, natural skin texture, realistic hair and clothing fibers, physically plausible light transport, accurate reflections, subtle lens imperfections, natural depth of field, restrained film grain, realistic motion blur, premium full-frame cinema-camera character, believable exposure and color, documentary-level environmental detail. The scene should look captured by a real camera rather than rendered.

## Negative direction

> Avoid CGI appearance, 3D render, videogame graphics, plastic skin, wax faces, perfect symmetry, excessive neon, holograms, floating UI, impossible reflections, inconsistent shadows, oversharpening, artificial bokeh, cartoon styling, illustration, synthetic-looking materials.

> Ordinary Lagos street at golden hour, real pedestrians and traffic, one central subject walking naturally, subtle environmental imperfections, cinematic 35mm composition, warm sunlight entering between buildings, realistic atmospheric haze and dust, shallow but physically plausible depth of field.

## Shot 02 seed

## Shot 03 seed

## Shot 04 seed
