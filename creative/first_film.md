# BlindLab — Reality Has a Glitch

## Creative rule

The scene must look **filmed**, while the central idea can look impossible.

If the viewer notices the rendering before noticing the idea, the shot fails.

## 15-second cut

| Time | Shot | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Ordinary Lagos street | Establish photographic reality |
| 3–6s | Subtle impossible event | Create curiosity |
| 6–10s | Close reveal | Make the impossible undeniable |
| 10–13s | Wide reveal | Show scale |
| 13–15s | BlindLab end card | Brand recall |

## Visual standard

- Full-frame cinema-camera character
- 24/35/50/85mm lens language
- Natural depth of field
- Realistic motion blur
- Practical light sources
- Physically coherent reflections and shadows
- Natural skin, hair, fabric and material texture
- Restrained color grade and film grain
- No default neon-futuristic styling
- No plastic skin
- No floating holographic interfaces
- No obvious 3D-render aesthetic

## Review gate

Reject a frame if any of these dominate the image:

1. synthetic faces or anatomy
2. impossible reflections
3. inconsistent shadows
4. waxy/plastic materials
5. excessive sharpening
6. game-engine/CGI appearance
7. unexplained camera physics

The goal is not to prove that an image is real. The goal is to make the **fiction visually credible**.
