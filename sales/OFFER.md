# BlindLab Car Ad Studio

## Promise

Send us your car photos and basic details. We turn them into a polished advertising package for WhatsApp, Instagram and other social channels.

## Starter offer

- 3 advertising concepts
- 3 social-ready image prompts/creative directions
- 3 captions
- 1 WhatsApp Status version
- delivery after review

## Pilot pricing

Use a low-friction introductory price while validating demand. Do not advertise a fixed price publicly until the first customer conversations establish willingness to pay.

## Sales rule

Sell the finished advertising outcome. Do not lead with “AI”.

## Quality rule

Never invent a vehicle's mileage, condition, ownership history, price, location, phone number, logo, or specification. Customer-supplied facts are the source of truth.
